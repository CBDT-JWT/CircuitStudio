Privacy policy / 隐私政策

No developer data collection

Circuit Studio does not require an app account, include advertising or analytics, or track you across apps and websites.

Website downloads

The download form stores the email address you enter, request and download timestamps, and downloaded app version on the developer’s server. The public download count shows the number of distinct email addresses that have received a complete installer response. Email addresses are never shown publicly. A short-lived hashed request-source identifier limits repeated requests; the website server also keeps basic operational request logs. Email entries are retained as download records. Contact the support address below to request removal.

Software updates

The direct-download Mac app checks a signed release feed hosted on GitHub and downloads signed updates. Update checks do not send your circuit documents or simulation results. Sparkle system profiling is disabled. Automatic checking can be changed in app settings.

Your documents

Circuit diagrams, figures, labels, models and previews are stored in the files you create. We do not receive these files. If you save them in iCloud Drive, Apple handles storage and synchronization under your Apple account and settings.

Local simulation

SPICE analyses run on your device using the bundled ngspice engine. Circuit data and simulation results are not sent to a remote simulation service.

Export and sharing

You choose when and where to export or share. CircuitikZ exports include editable circuit data for re-import. Files shared with another app or service are handled according to that recipient's privacy practices.

Settings and file access

Appearance preferences are stored locally. File access is granted through system document pickers. Circuit Studio does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone or camera.

Support

Developer: Weitao Jiang. For support and privacy inquiries, contact cbdt@weitao-jiang.cn.

Support correspondence is voluntary and separate from app usage. Avoid including confidential circuit data unless you intend to share it with the developer.

中文说明

Circuit Studio 不要求注册应用账号，不包含广告或应用内统计追踪。电路、模型、图示与仿真结果在设备上处理。文件由你选择保存位置；选择 iCloud Drive 时，由 Apple 根据你的账号设置存储与同步。

导出与分享由你主动发起。CircuitikZ 文件包含用于重新导入的可编辑电路数据。应用只通过系统文档选择器访问你选择的文件，不请求通讯录、定位、相机或麦克风权限。界面偏好保存在本机。

官网下载前填写的邮箱、领取时间和下载版本会保存到开发者服务器。页面只显示按邮箱去重的下载人数，不公开邮箱。下载记录会被保留；如需删除，请联系下方支持邮箱。请求来源的短期哈希标识用于限制重复请求，网站服务器也保留基础运行日志。直接下载的 Mac 版通过 GitHub 上的签名更新源检查和下载新版，不上传电路或仿真数据；可在设置中调整自动检查。

主动向支持人员发送的邮件及附件由你决定；请避免包含不希望分享的电路或研究信息。如需支持或咨询隐私问题，请联系 cbdt@weitao-jiang.cn。
