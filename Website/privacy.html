Privacy policy / 隐私政策

No tracking

Drawing and simulation do not require an app account. Circuit Studio does not include advertising or analytics, or track you across apps and websites.

Website downloads

The download form stores the email address you enter, request and download timestamps, and downloaded app version on the developer’s server. The public download count shows the number of distinct email addresses that have received a complete installer response. Email addresses are never shown publicly. A short-lived hashed request-source identifier limits repeated requests; the website server also keeps basic operational request logs. Email entries are retained as download records. Contact the support address below to request removal.

Software updates

The direct-download Mac app checks a signed release feed hosted on GitHub and downloads signed updates. Update checks do not send your circuit documents or simulation results. Sparkle system profiling is disabled. Automatic checking can be changed in app settings.

Your documents

Circuit diagrams, figures, labels, models and previews are stored in the files you create. Cloud AI features send only the context or image you explicitly choose to analyze. If you save them in iCloud Drive, Apple handles storage and synchronization under your Apple account and settings.

Local simulation

SPICE analyses run on your device using the bundled ngspice engine. Circuit data and simulation results are not sent to a remote simulation service.

CircuitAI and subscriptions

Drawing and SPICE simulation run locally. CircuitAI is optional and requires your consent before sending information through the developer relay to DeepSeek. For each request, this can include the active circuit connections and parameters, relevant simulation samples and waveform images, messages, AI tool results and images you attach. The AI can propose or execute edits and run local simulations in response to your request. Review its changes and results.

The relay stores subscription identifiers, usage counters, request identifiers and hashes, and successful AI replies or tool arguments for duplicate-request protection. AI replies may repeat circuit or message information. Request payloads, source images and full circuit documents are not written to the relay database. Cached reply entries older than 24 hours are removed when further requests are processed. Basic operational server logs may contain request metadata. DeepSeek processes submitted content under its own privacy practices. Apple handles subscription payments; the developer does not receive payment-card details. Contact the support address below about access or deletion of developer-held records.

Export and sharing

You choose when and where to export or share. CircuitikZ exports include editable circuit data for re-import. Files shared with another app or service are handled according to that recipient's privacy practices.

Settings and file access

Appearance preferences are stored locally. File access is granted through system document pickers. Circuit Studio does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone or camera.

Support

Developer: Weitao Jiang. For support and privacy inquiries, contact cbdt@weitao-jiang.cn.

Support correspondence is voluntary and separate from app usage. Avoid including confidential circuit data unless you intend to share it with the developer.

CircuitAI 为可选功能。首次使用前需要你同意：当前电路连接与参数、与请求有关的仿真数据和波形图、对话消息、AI 工具结果及你添加的图片，会经开发者服务器转交 DeepSeek 处理。AI 可以按你的请求修改电路并运行本地仿真，请核验修改与分析结果。

服务器保存订阅标识、调用计数、请求标识与哈希，以及用于防止重复请求的成功回答或工具参数。回答可能复述电路或对话中的信息。请求原文、源图片与完整电路文件不写入中转服务数据库。超过 24 小时的回答缓存会在后续请求处理中清理，运行日志可能保留基础请求元数据。DeepSeek 按其自身隐私规则处理提交内容。订阅付款由 Apple 处理，开发者不会收到银行卡信息。关于开发者持有记录的访问或删除，可联系下方支持邮箱。

导出与分享由你主动发起。CircuitikZ 文件包含用于重新导入的可编辑电路数据。应用只通过系统文档选择器访问你选择的文件，不请求通讯录、定位、相机或麦克风权限。界面偏好保存在本机。

官网下载前填写的邮箱、领取时间和下载版本会保存到开发者服务器。页面只显示按邮箱去重的下载人数，不公开邮箱。下载记录会被保留；如需删除，请联系下方支持邮箱。请求来源的短期哈希标识用于限制重复请求，网站服务器也保留基础运行日志。直接下载的 Mac 版通过 GitHub 上的签名更新源检查和下载新版，不上传电路或仿真数据；可在设置中调整自动检查。

主动向支持人员发送的邮件及附件由你决定；请避免包含不希望分享的电路或研究信息。如需支持或咨询隐私问题，请联系 cbdt@weitao-jiang.cn。
